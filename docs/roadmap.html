Roadmap & vision

What is built, what is next, and where it goes.

Both bundles run end to end: deploy, run, read the leaderboard. They are harnesses, so several trainers, evaluators, and gold features are still stubs. This page is the honest ledger of the work left, drawn from the TODOs in the source, and then the wider picture of where the pattern reuses.

obj_recg · status

Computer vision, built vs. next

Built

  • Bundle config, four medallion schemas, a managed volume, and a serverless Lakeflow pipeline.
  • Landing, bronze, silver, and gold transforms with quality expectations.
  • A model zoo of six families and nine specs, family-based dispatch, one MLflow experiment per family, and Unity Catalog registration with a @prod alias.
  • The Ultralytics (YOLO and RT-DETR) train-and-validate path and the comparison-table writer.
  • A team-performance XGBoost scaffold across seven targets.

Next, from the source TODOs

  • data Stage COCO annotations, frames, and game CSVs per sport, and confirm the COCO and game-result field paths in silver.
  • train The torchvision detection training loop and its COCOeval loop (mAP and OKS for the R-CNN and CNN families).
  • train YOLO and RT-DETR per-image label conversion, and Fast R-CNN external-proposal feeding.
  • eval MMPose training and evaluation, MediaPipe evaluation, and the true 3D error metric once 3D ground truth exists.
  • gold athlete_metrics: biomechanics angles, an athlete_id roster link, and a player_rating label, which unlock the player-index and scholarship-ROI targets.
  • gold team_game_features: join aggregated athlete metrics for CV-derived team-strength features.

Vision · the reusable core

One pattern, many programs

Golf swings and graduate salaries are the examples, not the point. The point is a repeatable way to answer "which approach is best for this task, on our data, under our governance," and that pattern transfers cleanly across education and the wider public sector.

Both bundles are the same machine in different clothes: a registry of approaches, a medallion flow that feeds them, MLflow to record every run, Unity Catalog to govern the winners, and serverless compute so nobody manages a cluster. Point it at a new task and the machinery does not change.

extend

Registry-driven

A new model, dataset, or reducer is a one-line append. Dispatch, evaluation, and the leaderboard pick it up.

trust

Governed

Every run logs to MLflow; champions register to Unity Catalog with a @prod alias and full lineage.

scale

Serverless

GPU for vision, CPU for tabular. No cluster to babysit, and the DR bundle also runs off-platform.

shape

Medallion

Bronze, silver, gold keeps raw data auditable and the training inputs clean and reproducible.

Repurposing the vision bundle

Detection and pose, beyond athletics

The vision bundle already parameterizes by sport. Swap the detection classes, keypoints, and labels, and the same detect-then-predict pipeline serves programs that have nothing to do with a scoreboard.

higher-ed athletics

Recruiting & development

Golf today, any of six sports tomorrow. Pose and biomechanics feed athlete-index and scholarship-ROI models: evidence for recruiting decisions and budget defense.

kinesiology · rehab

Movement assessment

The same 2D and 3D pose models measure gait, range of motion, and rehabilitation progress for kinesiology programs, athletic training, and adaptive PE. A benchmark picks the model accurate enough for clinical use.

campus & facilities

Safety & utilization

Object detection for occupancy and crowd flow, lab-equipment use, PPE compliance, and parking. The leaderboard picks the detector that hits the latency budget on the available hardware.

state & local

Infrastructure & access

Pedestrian and vehicle detection for transit and traffic studies, or ADA-access audits. The one-stage versus two-stage tradeoff the bundle measures is exactly the choice these deployments face.

Repurposing the DR bundle

Outcome analytics, beyond earnings

The dimensionality-reduction harness is domain-agnostic. Any wide administrative or survey table with an outcome to predict and levers to interrogate fits the same grid.

student success

Retention & persistence

Predict which students are at risk and which supports co-move with persistence. PCA and clustering turn a wide student record into a few actionable cohorts and factors.

enrollment · aid

Enrollment & financial aid

Model yield and the aid packages associated with it. The reducer tells you how many independent factors a strategy really rests on.

workforce

Labor-market alignment

The ACS pathway already sits in the bundle. Extend it to ask which credentials and fields align with regional wage outcomes for workforce-development programs.

public programs

Program outcomes

State and local benefits, training, and housing programs share the shape: wide records, a binary outcome, and a need to know which actions matter. Same harness, new dataset spec.

How you extend it

A new use case is a new spec, not a new codebase

Because each axis is a registry, standing up a new task is mostly declaration. For the DR bundle:

# src/dr_bench/datasets.py — one new DatasetSpec, then it joins the grid
DatasetSpec(
    name="retention_2026",
    loader="warehouse_table",
    target_col=["persisted_next_term"],
    numeric_cols=["credits_attempted", "gpa", "aid_amount", "advising_visits"],
    categorical_cols=["program", "cohort", "first_gen"],
    target_kind="label_equals",
)

Add its key to the sweep config and the 4 × 4 × 2 × 6 grid expands to include it: every reducer and model, cross-validated, on the leaderboard. Adding a CV sport or a new model family follows the same one-entry principle.

The responsibility that comes with it

Outcomes about people demand care

Non-negotiable for education data

These techniques touch students, minors, and protected characteristics. Prediction is association, not cause: a factor that co-moves with an outcome is a place to investigate, not a verdict on a person. Any repurposing should keep demographic attributes for fairness auditing rather than targeting, run under Unity Catalog access controls and lineage, respect FERPA and equivalent privacy law, and pair the harness with human review before any decision reaches a student or citizen.

Start from the examples, then take the pattern where your program needs it.
